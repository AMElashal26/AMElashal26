# Landing page goals and rubric

## Primary outcomes
1. Attract collaborators aligned with automation, data workflows, and LLM reliability.
2. Surface top repositories in under 15 seconds.
3. Demonstrate credibility through shipped work and activity signals.
4. Convert visitors into action (issue, discussion, follow, or outreach).

## Evaluation rubric (score each template 1–5)
- **Clarity**: Is value proposition obvious in one glance?
- **Personality**: Does the profile sound like AM Elashal, not a generic resume?
- **Proof-of-work**: Are outcomes, artifacts, and repository strength visible?
- **Discoverability**: Can visitors quickly decide where to click next?
- **Maintenance effort**: Can this stay fresh with low manual overhead?
